What AIS-OS is

The AIS-OS is a practical kit—based on Nate Herk’s MIT framework—for building your AI Operating System inside Claude Code. It organizes everything into two complementary frameworks and three ready-to-use skills.

3 Ms Framework

The operator’s brain: how you think, decide, and build.

  • 🧠Mindset — how to think of AI as an operator
  • 🧭Method — how to decide what to automate
  • 🛠️Machine — how to build and operate

4 Cs Framework

The system architecture: the four pillars of an AIOS.

  • 🗂️Context — knows your business
  • 🔌Connections — reaches your tools
  • 🧩Capabilities — knows how to do the work
  • ⏰Cadence — runs without being asked

AIS-OS Litmus Test

"While you’re away from your desk, your AIS-OS observes a real event and produces an output faster and more accurately than you would."

If this still isn’t happening, the AIS-OS was only half built.

Boring is Beautiful · Workflows beat agents

The AIS-OS isn’t about impressing people with sophisticated agents. It’s about building predictable, deterministic, boring workflows that work every time without supervision. The beauty is in consistency, not complexity.

How to know it worked

📲

Team reaches out

Your team starts asking you, "How do you do that so fast?" — a sign that the system is visible and working.

🧘

Context-switching reduction

You stop switching between tabs and tools all the time. The system searches and synthesizes for you before you even ask.

🧠

Quick start

From zero to a running AIOS in 14 days.

1

Clone the repository and open it in Claude Code

github.com/inematds/ais-os — clone locally, open the folder in Claude Code. The kit comes with the skills ready to use.

2

Run /onboard (7 questions)

Claude asks 7 questions about your business and generates the foundational context for your AIOS. It takes ~15 minutes.

3

Use it for 1 week with real questions

Work as usual using Claude Code for real day-to-day tasks. Let the system learn through use.

4

Day 7 — run /audit

Four-Cs Diagnosis: Claude assesses each pillar and identifies the system’s main bottleneck. Generates a health report.

5

Day 14 — run /level-up

Based on the audit, plan and implement 1 new automation. No more than that — consistency beats complexity.

6

Weekly ritual — 1 artifact per week

Every week: 1 new artifact (prompt, workflow, or context document) enters the system. The AIOS grows sustainably and without clutter.
